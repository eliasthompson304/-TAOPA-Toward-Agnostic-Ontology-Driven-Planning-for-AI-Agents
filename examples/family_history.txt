The Cedar Valley FamilySearch Center serves walk-in visitors, returning family historians, and scheduled community groups. The center coordinator, Morgan Hale, oversees daily operations, supervises six volunteer consultants, publishes open hours, prepares training sessions, and reviews monthly center activity. A family history consultant helps patrons locate relatives, interpret historical documents, and learn how to maintain their family trees. An experienced research consultant can also review difficult identity matches and conflicting evidence. A technology specialist handles computer and network issues, while a facilities contact handles lighting, furniture, accessibility, and building repairs. The coordinator may ask the volunteer scheduling lead to adjust shifts when a consultant is absent. Volunteers may teach guests how to use resources but must not impersonate guests or use guest account credentials. Patrons control changes to their own family tree information. Center computers, microfilm readers, flatbed scanners, and document cameras are shared resources. A research consultation, digitization appointment, training workshop, and equipment maintenance visit are different types of scheduled activity.
